## NOTES

1. **Login Credentials**: After registration, the Anchor will use the **Official Email Address** (info@greenvalleyagro.com) and **Password** to log in to the portal.

2. **File Uploads**: The system accepts the following file formats:
   - ID Document: .pdf, .jpg, .jpeg, .png
   - Organization Logo: .jpg, .jpeg, .png
   - Certificate of Incorporation: .pdf, .jpg, .jpeg, .png

3. **Areas of Operation**: At least one state must be selected. You can select multiple states or use the "Select All" button to choose all 37 states.

4. **Registration Flow**: 
   - Individual Registration: 3 steps (no password/security step)
   - Company Registration: 4 steps (includes password/security step)

5. **Approval Process**: After submission, the registration goes to the Coordinating Agency for approval. The Anchor can track the status by logging in with their credentials.

6. **Required vs Optional Fields**: Fields marked with * are required. Optional fields include WhatsApp, Website, Social Media handles, and Organization Logo.
